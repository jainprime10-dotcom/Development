# Product Brief

## Business direction
A premium digital-first event experiences company. Initial market: India, beginning with Jaipur. International expansion is a later stage.

## Initial services
- Wedding experiences and event coordination
- Digital wedding invitations and wedding websites
- Corporate events and conferences
- Private celebrations
- Event technology as a future product line

## Initial website objective
Convert qualified visitors into enquiries. The first release is a polished marketing website, not a full booking SaaS.

## Target audiences
- Couples and families planning weddings
- Corporate teams and businesses
- Customers seeking digital invitations and wedding websites
- Event partners and venues

## Brand status
Final brand name, domain and trademark clearance are pending. Use `BRAND_NAME_PENDING` in code and neutral placeholders in designs until approved.

## Visual direction
Warm luxury: ivory, champagne, charcoal, restrained metallic accents, editorial serif typography, generous whitespace, carefully art-directed photography.

## Trust and accuracy
Do not fabricate portfolio projects, client logos, testimonials, awards, venue partnerships, prices or business credentials. Clearly label concept/sample invitation work.
